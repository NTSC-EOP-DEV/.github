# NTSC-EOP-GROUP

中国科学院国家授时中心 **EOP组**（NTSC-EOP-GROUP）。
**EOP Group**, National Time Service Center (NTSC), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

面向地球定向参数（Earth Orientation Parameters, EOP）的测量与服务、VLBI科学与技术、空间大地测量研究，开展数据方法、软件开发、科研教学。
We focus on the measurement and service of Earth Orientation Parameters (EOP), VLBI science and technology, and space geodesy research, conducting data methods, software development, and research & teaching.

## 研究课题 / Research Topics

- **VLBI 世界时（UT1）测定 / VLBI UT1 Determination**：VLBI 观测数据处理与 UT1 高精度确定
  VLBI observation data processing and high-precision UT1 determination.
- **EOP 融合与预报 / EOP Combination and Forecasting**：地球定向参数的融合与预报算法
  Combination and forecasting algorithms for Earth orientation parameters.
- **地球自转变化动力学机制 / Dynamics of Earth Rotation Variations**：地球自转变化背后的地球动力学机制研究
  Study of geodynamic mechanisms behind Earth rotation variations.
- **脉冲星VLBI观测 / Pulsar VLBI Observations**：脉冲星的VLBI观测，时间尺度、时间同步
  VLBI observations of pulsars, time scales, and time synchronization.
